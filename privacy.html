Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information SaleSpark (“SaleSpark”, “we”) collects when businesses use our WhatsApp marketing and lead management service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who this policy covers

Our customers are businesses. When a business uses SaleSpark to talk to its own customers, that business decides what information to collect and why. We process that information on the business’s behalf, to provide the service.

If you are a customer of a business that uses SaleSpark, please contact that business first about your information. We will help them respond to you.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, business name and team members. Passwords are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that cannot be read back.
  • WhatsApp Business information, when a business connects its number through Meta: the WhatsApp Business Account and phone number identifiers, an access token (stored encrypted), and the messages sent and received through the number, with their delivery and read statuses.
  • Contacts that a business adds or imports: names, phone numbers, tags and whether each person agreed to receive marketing messages.
  • Facebook Page and ad information, when a business connects them: Page and ad account names, ad results (such as reach, clicks, spend and leads), and the answers people submit in the business’s lead forms.
  • Information the business adds for its AI assistant, such as business details and frequently asked questions.
  • Technical information: basic logs of requests to our service (such as time, page and IP address) used for security and troubleshooting, and a single login cookie that keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use information

  • To provide the service: sending and receiving WhatsApp messages, running campaigns and automatic replies, organising leads and showing reports.
  • To generate AI replies, only when a business turns the AI assistant on (see below).
  • To keep accounts and the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To provide support, and to contact account owners about their account and important changes.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the information businesses store in SaleSpark to show advertising.

AI assistant

If a business turns on the AI assistant, the text of customer messages in a chat, together with the business’s own information and FAQs, is sent to an AI provider to write a reply. The provider is Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude), as configured by SaleSpark. The AI is instructed to answer only from the business’s information and to hand the chat to a person when unsure.

Who we share information with

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram), to send and receive messages and to access Pages, ads and lead forms that a business has connected.
  • AI providers (Google, Anthropic), only for businesses that turn on the AI assistant.
  • Service providers who host and run SaleSpark for us (such as hosting, database and email providers), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

Information we receive from Meta’s platforms is used only to provide SaleSpark’s features to the business that connected it, as permitted by Meta’s Platform Terms. We do not transfer it to data brokers or use it for any other purpose.

How long we keep information

We keep information while the business account is active. When an account is closed or a deletion request is confirmed, we delete the related information within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example invoices).

Security

We use HTTPS for all connections, encrypt stored access tokens, hash passwords, check that WhatsApp webhooks really come from Meta, and limit staff access to what is needed to run and support the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected businesses of any breach as required by law.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent, by emailing salesparkbusiness.official@gmail.com. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also raise a grievance with us at the same address, and we will respond within 30 days.

To delete your data, see our Data Deletion page.

Children

SaleSpark is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Where information is processed

Our service providers, including Meta and AI providers, may process information outside India. We use providers who protect it with appropriate safeguards.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will show the new date at the top. For important changes we will notify account owners.

Questions or requests: salesparkbusiness.official@gmail.com.
