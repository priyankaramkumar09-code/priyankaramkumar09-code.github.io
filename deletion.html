Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 23 November 2026

You can ask SaleSpark to delete your data at any time. Here is how.

If you have a SaleSpark account

  • Email salesparkbusiness.official@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my SaleSpark data”.
  • Tell us whether you want your whole business account deleted, or only your own user login.
  • We confirm the request by replying to that email, then delete the data within 30 days and email you when it is done.

If you connected SaleSpark with Facebook

  • You can remove SaleSpark’s access at any time: in Meta Business Suite go to Settings, then Integrations, then Connected apps, and remove SaleSpark. Or in Facebook, go to Settings, then Business integrations.
  • Removing access stops SaleSpark from receiving new data. To also delete what we already stored, send the email above.

If you are a customer of a business that uses SaleSpark

Ask the business you were in contact with to delete your information; they control it. If you cannot reach them, email us with the business name and your phone number and we will pass on your request and help them complete it.

What is deleted

Account and team details, contacts, chats and messages, templates, campaigns, leads, AI assistant information, and stored access tokens for connected WhatsApp, Facebook and ad accounts. We keep only records the law requires, such as invoices.
